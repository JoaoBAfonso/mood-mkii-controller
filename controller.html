Connection and values +
  1. Use Chrome or Edge. Connect USB–MIDI to the ¼″ TRS MIDI IN using a compatible converter such as MIDIBox.
  2. Choose the pedal’s output and channel (default: 2). Select the connected model.
  3. To change the pedal’s channel, hold both footswitches at power-up and send a Program Change on that channel.
